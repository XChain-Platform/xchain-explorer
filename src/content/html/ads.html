Advertise on XChain

If you would like to advertise on XChain.io, please send an email to advertise@xchain.io with the following information :

  • icon - 20x20 pixels in gif/jpg/png format
  • text - up to 140 characters
  • site - site you would like linked
  • budget - amount you are looking to spend
  • timeframe - length of time that you would be looking to run the ad

Once we have received the above information, we will review your ad request and get back to you with additional information

Note: We also offer free and discounted advertising services to counterparty projects which we feel are worthwhile.
