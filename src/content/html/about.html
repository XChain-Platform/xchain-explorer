The XChain Platform is an open source token platform for any blockchain. It is live today on Bitcoin, Litecoin, and Dogecoin, with no sidechains and no bridges: one protocol carries tokens, a decentralized exchange (DEX), dispensers, smart contracts, staking, oracles, and cross-chain calls, and your app targets XChain rather than any single chain.

XChain is released under the GNU Affero General Public License v3.0: free to use, study, run, and build on. The code, documentation, and white paper are all public, and this explorer is itself one of the platform's open source components.
